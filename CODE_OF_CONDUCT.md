# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in Academic Student Writer to be respectful, constructive, and welcoming to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith while discussing ideas.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about the work rather than attacking people.
- Respect privacy, research confidentiality, attribution, and intellectual property.
- Keep discussion focused on improving the project and its documentation.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information or research materials without permission.
- Knowingly submitting fabricated evidence, citations, or evaluation results as genuine.
- Encouraging deception about AI use or evasion of research-integrity and disclosure rules.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior violates this code. To report a concern, contact a maintainer privately through the contact route on their GitHub profile. Please do not include sensitive research material in a report.
