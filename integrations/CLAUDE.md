## Academic writing

For any research paper, thesis, report, abstract, LaTeX manuscript, or writing review, use the Academic Student Writer skill provided with this project.
Start by reading `SKILL.md` and follow its section 3 (read the whole project, build `PROJECT_BRIEF.md` and `VOICE_PROFILE.md`) before writing. Read the task-relevant supporting guides when they are available.
Never invent results, numbers, or citations. Never help evade AI detectors or disclosure rules.
